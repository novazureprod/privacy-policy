Ultra-dynamic cinematic shot. A majestic phoenix made of luminous blue and golden light
streaks in from the far left corner of a deep matte black frame, flying at high speed
toward the center. Its wings beat powerfully, leaving trails of light and small sparks.
The motion accelerates, with a sweeping, slightly curved flight path, then decelerates
and lands in the exact center of the frame, folding its wings into the final pose of the
logo shown in the reference image. The final phoenix must be the same as the reference image: identical shape, identical pose,
identical wing and tail feathers, identical head and beak, identical proportions and colors.
Do not reinterpret, redraw or stylize the logo; copy it exactly from the reference. The phoenix holds still for one second, lit by a soft metallic
blue reflection that glides across its surface like light on polished steel. Then the
reflection fades out, the phoenix dissolves gently into the black background, and the
frame goes fully black.
Style: premium, cinematic, sharp detail, metallic blue and gold palette, deep matte black
background, no text, no watermark, no background elements, no camera shake.
Camera: static at first, then a subtle push-in as the phoenix settles.
Duration: 4 seconds. Aspect ratio 16:9.

- Joindre le logo de référence (PNG) comme image d'entrée, sinon la silhouette sera approximative.
- Fond noir mat uni : il permet de superposer la vidéo sur le site en mode « écran »
  (`mix-blend-mode: screen`), sans fichier à transparence.
- Le plan final doit correspondre à la position du logo sur le site. Vérifier la dernière image
  avant de l'intégrer.
- Exporter en MP4 (H.264) pour la compatibilité, puis une version WebM plus légère si besoin.
